\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}
Prior research in quantized language modeling focuses on weight-only and weight-activation quantization.
Recent advances demonstrate that 4-bit and 8-bit quantization preserve model perplexity on large benchmarks.
Literature in embedded systems shows specialized kernel optimization for ARM Helium vector units.
We build upon foundational work in sparse attention and structured tensor pruning.
