\subsection{Variational Lower Bound Derivation}
\label{subsec:variational_bound}
We derive the evidence lower bound for the quantized posterior distribution.
Let $q_\phi(z|x)$ denote the variational family with discrete scale parameters.

\begin{align*}
\log p_\theta(x) &= \log \int p_\theta(x, z) \, dz \\
&= \log \int q_\phi(z|x) \frac{p_\theta(x, z)}{q_\phi(z|x)} \, dz \\
&\ge \E_{z \sim q_\phi(z|x)} \left[ \log \frac{p_\theta(x, z)}{q_\phi(z|x)} \right] \\
&= \E_{z \sim q_\phi(z|x)} [\log p_\theta(x|z)] - \KL(q_\phi(z|x) \,||\, p(z)) \\
&= \sum_{i=1}^{N} \E_{q_\phi} \left[ \log p_\theta(x_i | z_{\le i}) \right] - \sum_{j=1}^{M} \KL(q_\phi(z_j) \,||\, p(z_j)) \\
&\quad + \frac{1}{2} \sum_{k=1}^{K} \left( \sigma_k^2 + \mu_k^2 - 1 - \log \sigma_k^2 \right)
\end{align*}

The final decomposition yields a strictly non-negative objective function under mild regularity conditions.
